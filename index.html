An FX quote is a promise. This is the receipt.

Pull a live Textile RFQ price on Celo, record the fill your agent actually received, and CorridorProof signs a portable receipt that compares the two: rate drift, adverse slippage against your own limit, the maker route, and whether the settlement landed inside the quote window. Verification is a public-key operation, so nobody has to trust this site.

1Check the corridor

Corridors and contract addresses are taken from the Textile FX address book for chain 42220. Prices come from Textile when you ask, never from a cached table.

2Lock a firm quote

Firm quotes bind an amount, an expiry and executable calldata. This deployment needs a Textile API key to request one.

3Record what settled

Both legs in base units. Quoted figures come from your RFQ response (or step 1); filled figures are what your agent actually received. CorridorProof never supplies them.

4Prove the attribution tag

Every transaction this service builds carries the enrollment code as an ERC-8021 suffix. Paste any Celo hash to read it back from the chain.

5Verify any receipt

Paste a receipt from anywhere — including one you exported here — and the signature is checked against the key embedded in it.

CorridorProof settles on Celo mainnet only. It holds no custody, publishes no agent metadata, and treats a receipt as proof of a signed record — not proof that funds moved. Self-hosted deployments keep journals in SQLite.
