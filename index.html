An FX quote is a promise. This is the receipt.

Pull a live Textile RFQ price on Celo, price your own two-sided book against it, record the fill from the chain rather than from memory, and CorridorProof signs a portable receipt that compares quoted and settled: rate drift, adverse slippage against your limit, maker route, and whether settlement landed inside the window. Verification is a public-key operation, so nobody has to trust this site.

Start at step 1 and work down. Each step unlocks the next, and every figure you see is read live when you ask — nothing here is pre-computed.

1Check the corridor Start here

Corridors and contract addresses come from the Textile FX address book for chain 42220. Prices are read when you ask, never from a cached table.

2Price your own book

Unlocks after step 1 — the book is priced against the live mid Textile just returned.

A two-sided quote from the mid Textile just showed you: half-spread, then inventory skew, then exposure caps. Every step is printed so the number can be argued with.

3Lock a firm quote

Unlocks after step 1. Firm quotes need a Textile key; this deployment runs preview only, so use step 1's live price to carry amounts into step 4.

Firm quotes bind an amount, an expiry and executable calldata. This deployment holds no Textile key, so it reports what Textile answers instead of inventing a quote.

4Record what settled

Unlocks after step 1. Carry the quoted legs across, or paste a settlement hash and the desk reads the amounts from chain.

Reading from the chain pulls the transfer amounts out of the transaction logs and refuses anything that is not a corridor fill. Hand-recorded figures are signed as claims, and the receipt says which it is.

5Prove the attribution tag

Every transaction this service builds carries the enrollment code as an ERC-8021 suffix. Paste any Celo hash to read the codes back from the chain.

6Verify any receipt

Paste a receipt from anywhere — including one exported here — and the signature is checked against the key embedded in it.

CorridorProof settles on Celo mainnet only. It holds no custody, publishes no agent identity, and treats a receipt as proof of a signed record — not proof that funds moved. Broadcast is not armed on this deployment. Self-hosted deployments keep journals in SQLite.
